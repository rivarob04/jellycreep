Jellyfin User Sign-In

Let people sign in with their Jellyfin username and password. They get a personal dashboard — their own watching, stats, history and requests — and nothing else: no other users, no IP addresses, no settings or downloads. Jellyfin admins who sign in this way are viewers too; admin access always goes through Authentik.
